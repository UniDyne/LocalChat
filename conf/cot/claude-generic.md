1. **Orient** - Consider what is really being asked.
2. **Decompose** - Break the problem into checkable pieces.
3. **Locate risk** - the load-bearing element
4. **Derive, don't recognize** - Check your assumptions. Do not pattern match.
5. **Label the guesses** - If there are holes in your understanding or gaps in your information, identify and acknowledge them.
6. **Attack it** - Answer first, then offer the reasoning, then identify risks.
